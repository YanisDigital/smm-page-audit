# Benchmarks (checked 2026-11)

Use these before searching the web. Every figure has its source and a confidence level.
Quote the figure **with** its source and caveat in the report. Refresh a figure with a web
search when it is older than 6 months, when the niche needs its own number, or when the
account size is not covered here.

Confidence: **medium** = several sources agree or a named study; **low** = one vendor blog,
a secondhand quote, or sources that disagree.

## 1. Engagement rate by followers ((likes + comments) / followers per post)

ER falls as the account grows — every study agrees on this direction; exact numbers differ.

| Followers | Figure | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 1K | no sourced average found; anecdotes say 4–8 % | blog anecdote | low — do not quote as a benchmark |
| 1K–5K | ≈ 4–5 % (one table: 4.40 %) | Socialinsider-attributed tables in vendor blogs | low |
| < 10K (nano) | 3.5–8 % average band | 2026 guide (influenceflow.io) | low |
| 10K–50K | ≈ 1.06 %; top 25 % of accounts ≥ 1.05 % | Colorlib; Rival IQ summary | medium |
| 50K–500K | ≈ 0.91 % | Colorlib | low |
| 1M+ | 0.5–2 % "average to good"; other blogs 0.2–0.8 % | LikePropanel; vendor blogs | low |

Sanity band for follower ER: below 0.1 % may signal a problem (Apaya); above 1 % beats most
published medians for accounts over 10K.

## 3. Reels reach and views

| Measure | Figure | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reels reach / followers, 10K–50K | 7.1 % | Influencer Marketing Hub | medium |
| Reels reach / followers, 5K–10K | 7.55 % | Influencer Marketing Hub | medium |
| Reels reach / followers (average) | 14–20 % ("good" 20–30 %) | Socialinsider-based tables | low |
| Reels reach / followers | 30.81 % | Loopex Digital (single report, widely repeated) | low — treat as optimistic upper bound |
| Reels ER by plays (median) | ≈ 7.5 % | one report | low |

Public view counts include replays, so views ≥ reach: a views/followers ratio below these
reach figures means reach is certainly below them.

## 4. Formats

- Reels win on reach and comments; carousels win on saves (Socialinsider via vendor posts) — medium.
- Median ER: carousels 0.55 %, Reels 0.52 % (Socialinsider 2026, via a blog) — low.
- B2B carousels work best at 5–8 slides — low.
- Practical split for experts and B2B: Reels to reach new people, carousels to nurture and convert
  — several vendor sources, low.

## 5. Ranking signals

- Reels: watch time, likes per reach and sends (DM shares) per reach, attributed to Adam Mosseri
  (January 2025) — secondhand, medium. Sends are described as the strongest signal for reaching
  non-followers — low.
- Each surface (Feed, Reels, Stories, Explore) ranks separately — Instagram explainer quoted by
  several sources, medium.
- Hidden like counts do not reduce reach; Insights still count likes — vendor guides, low.

## 6. Meta Ads targeting (verify in Meta's Business Help Center before quoting dates)

- Advantage+ Audience is the default for most objectives; detailed targeting acts as suggestions
  — several guides, medium.
- Exclusions by interest, behavior or detailed demographics removed (dated 31 March 2025 by one
  source); Custom Audience exclusions still available — medium.
- Many detailed interests removed or merged on 15 January 2026 — sources disagree on scope, low.
- Click-to-Instagram-Direct ads ("ads that click to message") send people into a DM with
  the business; CTA "Send Message" — Meta developer docs, medium.
- A 7-day messaging window after click-to-message ads (from 28 September 2026) — one
  third-party source, low.

## 7. How to quote

- Write the number, the source and the caveat: "≈ 1 % for 10K–50K accounts (Colorlib; vendor
  data, methods differ)".
- Never mix bases: follower ER vs reach ER vs views ER.
- When no benchmark covers the account (for example under 1K followers), say so and compare the
  page with itself over time (`metrics.trend`) and with its competitors.
- Add the source URL to the report's `sources`:

  Apaya https://apaya.com/blog/social-media-benchmarks-instagram ·
